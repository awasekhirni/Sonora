Id	Module Name	Description & Business Case
CORE MODULES		
M-C-01	Identity & Access Management (IAM)	Centralized system for user registration, authentication, authorization, and session management. The business case is to provide a secure, seamless, and personalized user experience from the start.
M-C-02	Content Ingestion & Management	The backbone for uploading, validating, transcoding, and managing the lifecycle of all audio assets (music, podcasts, audiobooks) and associated metadata.
M-C-03	Digital Rights & IP Registry	A single source of truth for all copyrights, ownership splits, and licensing agreements for every piece of content.
M-C-04	Royalty Calculation Engine	The core financial engine that accrues micro-royalties for every stream based on complex rules, user country, subscription tier, and rights splits.
M-C-05	Core Streaming & Player Service	Delivers low-latency, high-quality adaptive bitrate audio streams. Includes the core player with play, skip, save, and queue functionality.
M-C-06	User & Community Management	Manages user profiles, social connections, followers, and the foundational elements of the community, including voting and review systems.
M-C-07	Core Subscription & Billing	Handles tiered subscription plans, free trials, upgrades/downgrades, invoicing, and payment processing via multiple gateways.
M-C-08	Basic Analytics & Reporting	Provides essential dashboards for business health (users, revenue, streams) and creator earnings.
ADVANCED & DIFFERENTIATING MODULES		
M-A-01	AI-Powered Content Generation (Soundforge)	Enables generation of original music, audiobook narrations, and ethically-guided sacred content (Wazifa, Quran) via curated AI models.
M-A-02	Advanced Personalization & Workspaces	Goes beyond basic algorithms. Allows users to create, fine-tune, save, and share custom recommendation algorithms in personal "Discovery Workspaces."
M-A-03	Community Curation & Voting Engine	Powers the "community-driven" aspect, allowing users to vote on content, influencing charts, playlists, and artist promotion through verified, fraud-resistant mechanisms.
M-A-04	Creator Monetization & Payout Portal	A comprehensive interface for creators to view granular earnings, manage payout thresholds/bank details, and raise disputes. Facilitates the automated monthly 70% payout.
M-A-05	Live Audio & Debate Studio	Facilitates live audio events, debates, and listening parties with real-time interaction, moderation tools, and the ability to archive and monetize recordings.
SUPPORTING & ENTERPRISE MODULES		
M-S-01	Data Governance & Lifecycle Mgmt	Establishes policies for data quality, classification, lineage, retention, and deletion to ensure compliance, security, and trustworthiness.
M-S-02	Data Ingestion & Quality Framework	A robust, scalable pipeline for ingesting all data (audio, logs, user events) with built-in validation, transformation, and quality checks.
M-S-03	Data Observability Platform	Provides monitoring, alerting, and root-cause analysis for data pipelines and assets, ensuring data is fresh, reliable, and accessible.
M-S-04	KPI & Success Metrics Framework	A centralized system to define, track, and visualize key performance indicators across the entire business, from user engagement to financial health.
M-S-05	Advertising & Sponsorship Engine	Manages programmatic and direct-sold audio ads, dynamic ad insertion, sponsorhips for podcasts/playlists, and campaign performance analytics.
M-S-06	Advanced Search & Discovery Index	Powers rich, faceted search across the entire catalog (by lyric, mood, instrumentation, guest) using NLP and audio analysis.
M-S-07	Global Content Delivery & CDN	A globally distributed network to deliver audio streams with low latency and high reliability, regardless of the user's location.
M-S-08	System Health & DevOps Observability	Monitors the health, performance, and security of all application infrastructure and services in real-time.
